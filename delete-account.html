Delete your account

Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

In the app

  1. Open Stillpress and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Account.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

You are signed out at once, and the account is gone.

What is deleted

  • Your account and email address.
  • The answers you gave when you set the app up.
  • Your plan record and your credit ledger. Unused credits are lost.
  • Every AI job record, every photo you uploaded for an AI effect, and every result.
  • Every cloud backup.

Edits and projects that are only on your phone are not touched; delete the app to remove them.

What is not

A subscription is a contract with Apple, not with us. Deleting your account does not cancel it: in your iPhone’s Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and cancel AI Plus there. Apple and RevenueCat keep their own purchase records under their policies.

If you cannot open the app

Email muneebrafishakoor@gmail.com from the address you signed in with, and we will delete the account for you and confirm by reply.
