Stillpress

Privacy Policy

Last updated September 8, 2026

Stillpress is a photo and video editor for iPhone made by Muneeb Rafi (“we”, “us”). This policy says what the app stores, what leaves your phone and when, and how to delete it. It is written to match what the app actually does; when the app changes, this page changes with it.

The short version

  • Editing, presets and export happen on your phone. Opening, editing and saving a photo or video uploads nothing.
  • Only three things send data anywhere: signing in, running an AI effect, and turning on cloud backup. Each is your choice.
  • There are no advertising or analytics SDKs in the app. We do not sell data, and we do not use your photos to train AI models.
  • You can delete your account and everything stored with it from inside the app, in one step.

Editing happens on your device

The editor, every preset and effect, and the export all run on your phone. The app reads only the photos and videos you choose to share with it through the iOS picker, and saves your finished edits back to your photo library when you ask it to. None of this involves our servers.

Your account

Editing, presets and export need no account. An account is needed only for AI effects, purchases and cloud backup. You sign in with your email address: we send a one-time code to it, and the address is what identifies your account.

Stored with your account:

  • Your email address.
  • The answers you gave when you first set the app up (who you make things for, what you make most, gender, age band, how comfortable you are with editing, the styles and topics you picked). They personalise the home feed and are not used for anything else.
  • Your plan (free or AI Plus) and a ledger of your credits: every grant and every spend, so your balance is always a sum we can show you.

AI effects

AI effects run in the cloud, not on your phone. The app tells you this before the first one runs and asks you to agree. When you run one:

  • The photo you are editing is uploaded to our storage under your account. For Remove, the selection you painted is uploaded with it. Nothing else on your device is sent.
  • Our server hands the photo to our AI provider, fal.ai, through a short-lived link, to run the model. fal.ai processes it only to return the result, under its own privacy policy.
  • The result is copied into our storage, so it survives the provider’s short retention, and sent back to your phone.
  • We keep a record of each job: which effect, when, whether it succeeded, and the credits it cost. A job that fails is refunded automatically.

Uploads and results stay with your account until you delete the account. AI effects are for people aged 18 or older, which is a condition of the provider’s terms; the app asks you to confirm this before the first run.

Purchases

Subscriptions and credit packs are sold and billed by Apple through your Apple account. We never see your card or bank details. RevenueCat validates your receipt and tells our server what you bought (the product, the dates, renewals and expiry), tied to your account id, so that your plan and credits appear on every device you sign in on.

Cloud backup

Cloud backup is an AI Plus feature and is off until you turn it on. When it is on, the projects you back up are stored with your account so you can restore them on another device. Turning it off stops new uploads; deleting a project, or the account, removes it.

Crash reports

If the app crashes, it may send a crash report to Sentry so we can fix it: the error, the steps inside the app that led to it, the build number and the device model. Reports never include your photos, file names, screenshots, your email or any other identifier. They are switched on only in builds where we have set them up.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Photos — to open the photos and videos you choose to edit, and to save your edits. On iOS you can share only a selection.
  • Camera and microphone — only when you shoot a photo or record a video inside the app. The app never records on its own.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs are bundled in the app.
  • We do not sell or rent your data, and we do not share it with anyone except the services named on this page, each of which acts only to provide its part of the app.
  • We do not use your photos, videos or results to train AI models.

Where your data lives

Account data, uploads, results and backups are stored with Supabase in its Mumbai (India) region. AI processing happens on fal.ai’s servers, purchase records at RevenueCat, and crash reports at Sentry, each in the regions those services operate. Data travels between your phone and these services over encrypted connections, and every row we hold is reachable only by your own signed-in session.

Retention and deletion

Everything stored with your account stays until you delete the account. Deleting it — in the app under Settings › Account › Delete account, or by emailing us — removes your account, your email address, your onboarding answers, your credit ledger, your AI job records, every upload and result, and every backup, at once. Purchases are recorded by Apple and RevenueCat under their own policies; deleting your account with us does not cancel a subscription, which you manage in your Apple ID settings. See how to delete your account.

Edits and projects that are only on your phone stay there until you delete them or the app.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of the data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or delete it, at any time by emailing muneebrafishakoor@gmail.com. If you are in a region with a data protection authority, you also have the right to complain to it.

Children

Stillpress is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. AI effects require you to be 18 or older. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

When the app starts doing something new with your data, this page is updated first and the date at the top changes.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: muneebrafishakoor@gmail.com.
