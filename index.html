Common questions

Do I need an account?

Not for editing. Every preset, effect and export works offline with no account. Only AI effects, purchases and cloud backup ask you to sign in, because they run on our servers.

How do credits work?

AI effects cost credits. AI Plus grants an allowance every billing period, and credit packs add more that never expire. Your balance is under Settings, and an effect that fails is refunded on its own.

I paid but my plan does not show.

Open Settings and tap Restore Purchases while signed in to the same Apple account you bought with. If it still does not appear, email us with the Apple receipt and we will fix it.

How do I cancel AI Plus?

In your iPhone’s Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Why do AI effects ask if I am 18?

AI effects are processed by our provider, fal.ai, whose terms require users to be 18 or older. Everything else in the app has no age condition beyond Apple’s.

Where do my photos go?

Nowhere, unless you run an AI effect or turn on cloud backup. The Privacy Policy lists exactly what leaves your phone and when.

Manage your account & data

You can delete your account and everything stored with it inside the app: Settings › Account › Delete account. Step-by-step instructions, and what to do if you cannot open the app.
